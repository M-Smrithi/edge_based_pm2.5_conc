Research platform

How much sensor data can an air-quality model afford to lose?

A lightweight PM2.5 estimator runs in a simulated edge gateway. We introduce 0%, 10%, and 20% additional missingness into sensor readings, using random and consecutive patterns, and measure the effects on prediction accuracy and processing time.

What this platform does

Estimates PM2.5
from 12 co-located sensors for the same hour — not a forecast.
Fills gaps locally
missing readings are replaced by medians learned from training data only.
Tests honestly
a frozen benchmark with a later period and unseen stations; nothing is tuned on it.
Learns from new data
administrators add station files; models are retrained, compared and promoted under explicit checks.
